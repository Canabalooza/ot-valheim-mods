# Changelog

## 1.0.1

- The Hood of the Vanguard now goes in an armor stand's head slot, from the
  panel or by using it from the hotbar. The game flags the hood to attach
  as a material, so vanilla stands refuse it.

## 1.0.0

Initial release.

- Use on an armor stand opens it like a chest, with one cell per slot laid
  out like the body.
- Empty cells show a faded black-and-white icon of what goes there and name
  the slot on hover.
- Take all empties the stand into your inventory.
- Swap armor trades the stand's armor for what you're wearing, from a
  button on the panel or Left Shift + E on the stand.
